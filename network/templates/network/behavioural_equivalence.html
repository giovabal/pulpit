How to read this page

Behavioural equivalence is the similarity of two channels' behavioural profiles: amplification, content originality, diffusion lag, and audience and activity volume (subscribers, messages). The volume features are log-scaled, so a few very large channels do not dominate; every feature is then min-max normalised, and a missing value is imputed to the median. Two channels score 1 when they behave alike (originate or amplify, fast or slow, narrow or wide), wherever they sit in the citation network.

The structural equivalence matrix asks the complementary question: which channels hold the same position.
