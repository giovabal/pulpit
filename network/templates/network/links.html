Where the channels send their readers. Every web link in the observed channels' messages — written in the text, hidden behind link text, on a button or in the link preview — is reduced to its site (the registered domain; each outlet on a hosting platform such as Substack is a site of its own) and filed under the categories of Manage → Domains. A site counts once per message; t.me links are citations and stay in the graph. Shares by role (monetisation, mainstream, alternative) count uncategorised links in the denominator, so they are lower bounds until every site is filed. Co-linking pairs share more sites than their activity and the sites' popularity explain (bipartite configuration model, exact p-values, Benjamini–Hochberg q over every pair); a link is a pointer the channel published, not a visit, a payment or an affiliation.
