Channels Network Back to map Method

Who dominates whom. A forward is a deferral: when X forwards Y, X took up Y's content and Y won that interaction. Channels are ranked by David's score (each pair's citations split between its sides in proportion to the dependence each places on the other, corrected for sparse dyads) and by SpringRank, and typed dominant (has satellites, is nobody's satellite), dependent (a satellite: at least half of its content or of its reach comes from one partner), broker (both), allied (neither, but in a validated mutual pair) or peripheral (none of these). Pairs carry each side's dependence on the other — content (share of its citing output) and reach (share of its received citations) — the net balance, and a validation of each link against a null of heterogeneous activity: alliance (both directions validated), dependence (one), unvalidated (none).
