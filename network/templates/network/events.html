Did the network change when the event happened? For each event recorded in Manage → Events, the weekly series around its date are fitted with a segmented regression: the level change is the jump at the event's week, the slope change the bend in the trend after it. An event scoped to labels is tested as a comparative series — the channels holding those labels against all the others — so a movement-wide shift does not count as the event's effect. p (HAC) uses autocorrelation-robust standard errors; p (placebo) is the share of other dates at which the same model finds an effect at least as large, and q corrects it for the number of events and metrics tested. Counts are modelled on log(1 + count): a level change of 0.1 is about +10%.
