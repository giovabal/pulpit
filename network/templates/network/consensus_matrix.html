How to read this page

Every pair of channels is compared across the community detection methods of this analysis: the more methods put the two channels in the same community, the larger and the redder the circle.

The channels are ordered by the community most methods give them. A group every method agrees on shows as a dense red block along the diagonal; small or blue circles mark the pairs only some methods join, the uncertain borders between communities.

Only independent detections count: label groups, k-core shells and the consensus partition itself are left out. Only the lower triangle is drawn. Hover a circle, or reach it with the keyboard, for the two channels and the number of methods that agree.
