How to read this page

Structural equivalence (Lorrain & White 1971) is the cosine between two channels' tie profiles: their weighted citations to, and from, every other channel. Two channels score 1 when they cite, and are cited by, the same channels with the same relative intensity: they hold interchangeable positions in the network, whether or not they cite each other. The pair's own tie is left out of both profiles.

The behavioural equivalence matrix asks the complementary question: which channels act alike, wherever they sit.
