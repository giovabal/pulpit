How to read this page

Each post's structural reach is measured twice. C (Goel et al. 2016) counts the distinct neighbouring communities whose channels re-forwarded the post; D (Cha et al. 2010) adds up the authority of the studied channels that forwarded it. The interest score is the post's engagement, z-scored within its own channel (Salganik 2006; Suh 2010), so a post is compared with its channel's usual level.

Only forwards made within the reaction window after the post count, so that anniversary and archive re-shares do not inflate a post's reach.
