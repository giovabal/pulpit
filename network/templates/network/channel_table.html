How to read this page

One row per channel. The first columns give its position by volume: in-strength, how much it is cited; out-strength, how much it cites; its subscribers and its messages. The table opens sorted by in-strength.

Influence measures read the whole pattern of citations: PageRank rates a channel by the standing of the channels citing it, and the HITS scores tell authorities, cited by many hubs, from hubs, citing many authorities.

Structural measures describe a channel's surroundings. Burt's constraint is low for a channel that bridges groups otherwise apart. The within-module z and the participation coefficient say whether a channel is a hub inside its community and how far its ties spread over the others; the Module role column sums the two up.

Content measures come from the messages: the share that is original rather than forwarded, how often the other channels forward the channel, and how many hours it usually takes to forward what others publish.

Every heading carries its definition as a tooltip, and a bracket after a name gives the setting the measure was computed with. A dash means the measure does not exist for that channel: a channel that is only cited, for instance, has no citations of its own to measure. The last columns give the community each method puts the channel in. With a timeline, the button beside a channel's name draws its measures period by period.

Every score is relative to the channels studied: it describes a channel's place among them, not on Telegram at large.
