Usage

The packs the crawler found in posts, reactions and groups' details. Mark in target the packs your study is about — an organisation's own pack, say; every pack starts out of target. The flag classifies a pack: the Emoji packs and Emoji pages and their CSVs can be filtered by it, and it never removes a pack's uses from a count. Uses and channels here count every stored post; the usage pages count in-target channels inside their in-target periods. A group pack is one a boosted group set as its own (recorded when the crawler fetches channel details). Changes save as you make them.
