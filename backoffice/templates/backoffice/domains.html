Domain categories

The kinds of web site the channels link to. The outbound-link analysis (structural_analysis --links) reports each channel's links by category, and three roles drive its share measures: Monetisation (donations, shops), Mainstream platform and Alternative platform. The starter list is a starting point: rename, recolour or delete any category; deleting one leaves its sites uncategorised. Changes save as you make them.

Sites

Every site the in-target channels link to inside their in-target periods — URLs in the text, hidden links, buttons and link previews; t.me links are citations and are not listed. A site is a registered domain (news.example.co.uk is example.co.uk), except on hosting platforms such as Substack or Shopify, where each outlet is a site of its own and starts in its platform's category. Counts are messages linking to the site, as of the last index (python manage.py index_domains, the crawl option Index link domains, or Rebuild index here).
