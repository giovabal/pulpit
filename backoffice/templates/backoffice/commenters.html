Commenter accounts

The user accounts that replied in the channels' discussion groups, read from every stored reply. Two kinds of account would distort the audience figures, such as the home page's unique commenters, so they are left out automatically: Telegram bots, as Telegram marks them, and bulk posters, accounts with {{ bulk_thread_comments }} or more replies under a single post, such as an archive dumped into one thread. Set Exclude to leave out an account the flags miss, such as a channel admin, or Keep to override a flag. Changes save as you make them. Telegram's bot flag is recorded as replies are fetched; for replies fetched earlier, run Refresh messages stats with Fetch replies.
