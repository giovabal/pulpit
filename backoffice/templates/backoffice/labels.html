Labels

Labels live in groups. A partition group holds at most one label per channel at any moment, so its periods can't overlap; non-partition groups allow concurrent labels. The single primary group supplies node colour, the “Label” export column, the vacancy actor identity, and the default community / role basis. A container group's labels are purely structural: they can't be assigned to channels — they only contain labels of other groups. E.g. a “Continents” container whose “Europe” label collects the Nation labels: pick a source group under the table, then drag each of its labels onto the right row (or click a label to choose). A container label can then be selected on the home page to scope the dashboard stats to the channels of its child labels.
